========================================================
VALUATION BASIS
========================================================

All physical damage claims (Collision and Comprehensive) are settled
on the basis of Actual Cash Value (ACV) at the time of loss, which
is the cost to replace the vehicle with one of similar make, model,
age, and condition, less applicable depreciation.

Total loss is declared when repair cost exceeds 80% of ACV.

========================================================
REQUIRED CLAIM DOCUMENTS
========================================================

To process any Collision or Upset claim the following documents
must be submitted within 30 days of the date of loss:

1. Completed Claim Form (NSI Form CA-001)
2. Police Report or Collision Reporting Centre Report
   (required for all collisions involving third parties or injuries)
3. Repair Estimate from a licensed repair facility
4. Proof of Ownership (vehicle registration or title)
5. Driver Licence (copy of licence of driver at time of loss)
6. Photographs of vehicle damage (minimum 4 photographs)

For theft claims, a police report is mandatory.
For glass-only claims, a repair estimate alone is sufficient.

========================================================
EXCLUSIONS AND RESTRICTIONS
========================================================

This policy does NOT cover loss or damage:

1. Arising while the insured vehicle is used for commercial delivery,
   transportation network company (TNC/rideshare) services, or
   for-hire transportation unless an endorsement is added.

2. Caused intentionally by the insured or any person operating
   the vehicle with the insured's consent.

3. Arising from racing, speed contests, or stunting on any road
   or track.

4. Occurring outside the territory of Canada and the continental
   United States (subject to the Out-of-Province extension clause).

5. To mechanical parts caused by mechanical breakdown, wear and
   tear, freezing, or gradual deterioration.

6. To personal belongings inside the vehicle (covered under
   homeowners or tenants policy).

7. While the driver's licence is suspended, expired, or the
   driver is excluded from coverage by endorsement.

8. Caused by war, invasion, rebellion, or nuclear incident.

========================================================
DUTIES AFTER A LOSS
========================================================

In the event of a loss, the insured must:

a) Promptly notify Northstar Insurance Company or its authorized agent.
b) Protect the vehicle from further damage.
c) File a police report within 24 hours where required.
d) Submit all required claim documents within 30 days of the loss date.
e) Cooperate fully with the company's investigation and inspection.
f) Submit to an Examination Under Oath if requested.

Failure to comply with these duties may result in denial of the claim.
