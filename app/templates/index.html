⚡ How it works

1. Policy extraction

AI reads your verified policy PDF and extracts coverage limits, deductibles, required documents, and exclusions.

2. Claim extraction

AI reads your claim document and extracts claim amount, loss description, VIN, and submitted documents.

3. Comparison

Deterministic Python rules compare every extracted fact against the active policy — no AI guesswork.

4. Outcome
